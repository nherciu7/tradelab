{{ideas_tested}} ideas are in the tally and {{ideas_rejected}} failed. The {{graveyard_listed}} below each have their own write-up in the research notes; most of the rest were variants and quick checks of these. Every idea was judged by the same four gates: the same direction in both halves of the data, a t-statistic above 2.8 (how unlikely a result is to be luck), beating its own benchmark, and a placebo on fake dates that fails.

A failure here means "didn't pass my test on my data after my costs". Some of these effects are real and simply too small for a small account. Trend following, for example, missed the bar narrowly and needs about €20,000 to trade with whole contracts.
